01 — The platform layer

Anthropic went first in May and chose distribution: put the workflow inside Claude and make every vendor a connector. Google went in August and chose integration: don't own the workflow, be the substrate, sell through the SIs. OpenAI went in September and chose content: own primary-law retrieval, then sell direct to the Am Law 200. Same market, three different bets about where the value sits.

Anthropic

Claude for Legal

Launched 2026-05-12

Distribution play: ship the legal workflow layer inside Claude itself, and make every incumbent a connector into it.

ModelClaude Opus 4.7 positioned as its most capable public model for legal reasoning and long-document work

  • 20+ MCP connectors into the software firms already run (DMS, CLM, e-discovery, research, deal rooms)
  • 12 practice-area plugins: Commercial, Corporate, Employment, Privacy, Product, Regulatory, AI Governance, IP, Litigation, plus Law Student, Legal Clinic, Legal Builder Hub
  • Each plugin ships a cold-start interview that learns the team's playbook, escalation matrix and house style
  • 4 plugins (Commercial, Corporate, Litigation, Product) also deploy as Managed Agents via the Claude API
  • Native in Microsoft 365 — Word (drafting/redlining), Outlook (triage), Excel (checklists), PowerPoint
  • Claude Cowork for multi-document work; Projects as persistent matter workspaces; scheduled tasks for recurring sweeps

AccessEnterprise via sales; discounted access for qualifying nonprofits through Claude for Nonprofits; A2J partnerships with Free Law Project and the Justice Technology Association

EcosystemHarvey, Legora, Thomson Reuters (CoCounsel), Relativity, Everlaw, Consilio, Ironclad, DocuSign, Definely, iManage, NetDocuments, Box, Datasite, Midpage, Trellis, Legal Data Hunter, Solve Intelligence, Crosby, Eve, Lawve AI, The L Suite (Lloyd, TopCounsel), BoardWise, Courtroom5, Descrybe, Free Law Project

Named firmsFreshfields, Accenture Legal, Holland & Knight, Quinn Emanuel, EvenUp, Crosby Legal

OpenAI

Astra for Law

Launched 2026-09-17

Content play: own the primary-law retrieval layer, then sell trusted access to the Am Law 200 direct.

ModelGPT-6 Astra, configured for legal work — not a separate model

  • Legal Search Index across 230M+ URLs of US case law, statutes, regulations, court rules and administrative decisions, built using CourtListener data
  • Legal-specific instructions for analysis and writing
  • 26 vendor plugins plus 9 community plugins (LegalQuants, LegalEng Consulting Group, Skills.law) and 47 user-built skills
  • ChatGPT for Word
  • Trusted Access program for Am Law 200 firms: zero data retention on the API, ChatGPT Enterprise excluded from human review; built with Latham & Watkins on permissions and ethical walls (~30-page agreement)

BenchmarksPassed correctness evals on 54% of legal research questions vs 38.7% for GPT-6 with web search alone; retrieved 24% more case law references and up to 54% more relevant passages

PeopleJason Boehmig, Ironclad co-founder, leads OpenAI's legal effort

EcosystemThomson Reuters, Harvey, Legora, iManage, Intapp, DeepJudge, Clio, Relativity

Named firms—

04 — Reading

Helen's Legal AI Lab — Helen Fan

The Legal AI Value Stack (V2): five levels from raw model, to workflow redesigned around agents, to a self-learning data layer within client and ethical-wall boundaries, to AI as system of record, to the AI-native firm. The most useful framework for judging whether a vendor has anything defensible.

LawSites — Bob Ambrogi

Still the fastest, most sceptical reporting on launches. His framing of the Anthropic launch — how much appetite does a model vendor have to compete in legal, and how do vendors built on it respond — is the open question of the year.

Artificial Lawyer

Europe-weighted, opinionated, early on the AI-native firm and regulatory-authorisation stories.
